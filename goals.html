Even when i was young, ive dream about travelling the world. I feel a certain feeling, or a seeing something glorius hidden to places i have never been before. That's why i promised to my self that i'll continue to learn new skills and develop experiences for my career.

I was raised by a solo parent, not earning the highest but gets us through the things we need everyday. Thats why i kept on spending my time, money, and mostly efforts on somethings we need so that when i graduate i can earn a mid or high-paying job and provide generational wealth for my family.

I’m not really a typical business guy, but the more i learn about it, the more i can see myself running one as a source of passive income. Although it may seem risky and requires a lot of experience, i believe it’s worth the try. Someday, i hope to build something of my own that combines my skills in technology with the lessons i’ve learned along the way.

Owning a house is everyone’s goal, and having one would certainly be one of the greatest achievements of my life. More than just a place to live, i want it to be a home where my family can feel safe and comfortable, especially my mother, who has given so much for me. Building it with them and for them is what motivates me to keep working hard every day.

A Good Provider

I want to be a good provider for my family, not just financially, but also with my time and effort. To me, being there for the people i love matters just as much as supporting them. Seeing them happy and secure because of what I’ve worked for is one of the things i want to achieve most in life.
